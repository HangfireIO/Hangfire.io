Patch release with fixed batch cancellation acknowledgment in background jobs from nested batches. Previously, such jobs continued to be processed by workers even if one of the parent batches in the hierarchy was canceled.

* **Fixed** – Support batch cancellation acknowledgement in background jobs from nested batches.
* **Fixed** – Pass timeout to Regex.IsMatch calls in _BatchHeader.cshtml.
